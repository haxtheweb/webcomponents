The building where I rent the room has a stairwell with one working bulb, on the landing between the second and third floors. Everything above and below it belongs to the dark. I count the steps on the way down because counting is something to do with the part of the mind that would otherwise be listening.

Twelve to the landing. Thirteen from the landing to the street door. Fourteen tonight. Thirteen. I counted again. Thirteen.

Writers like to pretend the work is a choice. It is closer to weather. Some nights the pressure drops and the pages come whether you are ready or not, and the only skill is to be at the desk when it happens, with paper loaded and the ribbon fresh.

I have started leaving the bulb's switch taped in the on position. The landlord has not noticed. Or he has, and he is leaving the tape there for a reason.
