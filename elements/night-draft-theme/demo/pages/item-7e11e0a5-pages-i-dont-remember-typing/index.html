Found three more pages in the drawer this morning. Same paper, same ribbon, the same slightly raised e that this machine has always had. My words, my rhythm. I have no memory of any of it.

The first page describes the lake below the cabin at four in the morning: the water so still it looks poured, a single light on the far shore that blinks out as I watch. I have never seen that light. There is no house on the far shore. I went down to check after breakfast. There is a house on the far shore.

The second page is a list of names. Some of them I know. One of them is mine, and someone has drawn a line through it, slowly, the way you would cross off a chore you were glad to finish.

The third page is blank except for one line near the bottom, centred, as if it were a title: keep typing.

So I am.
