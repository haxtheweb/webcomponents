The rain started a little after midnight and has not made up its mind since. I keep the lamp low so the window does not turn into a mirror. If it turns into a mirror I start looking at the man in it instead of the page.

Tonight the story wanted to go somewhere I did not plan. The detective was supposed to leave the house. He walked out into the street and the street was gone. He stayed. He sat down in the kitchen and waited for the kettle like a person who has nowhere better to be, and I let him, because I recognised the feeling.

There is a rule I made years ago: never stop on a full sentence. Stop in the middle of one so tomorrow has a handle to pick up. Tonight I am breaking it. Tonight the sentence ends, and the lamp stays on, and I sit with the quiet a while longer.

I am not afraid of the dark. I am afraid of what I type in it. I am tired. That is all this is.
