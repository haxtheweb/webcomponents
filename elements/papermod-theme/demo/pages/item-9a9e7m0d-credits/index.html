The layouts in this package pay homage to well known themes for Hugo, Jekyll and Astro, each released under the MIT license:

  • PaperMod, by Aditya Telange and nanxiaobei
  • Minima, by Parker Moore and the minima contributors
  • AstroPaper, by Sat Naing
  • Astro Cactus, by Chris Williams
  • Coder, by Luiz F. A. de Prá
  • Terminal, by panr
  • al-folio, by Maruan Al-Shedivat
  • AstroWind, by onWidget
  • Hyde, by Mark Otto
  • Chirpy, by Cotes Chung
  • Minimal Mistakes, by Michael Rose and contributors
  • Astrofy, by Manuel Ernesto Garcia

These themes are written for HAXcms from DDD design tokens and HAXcms theme parts. They use none of the code, styles or assets of the themes they honor, and are not affiliated with or endorsed by their authors.
