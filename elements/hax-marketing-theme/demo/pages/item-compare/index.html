How mature is the project behind it?

Features are half of a choice. The other half is whether the project will still be here. HEOSAT is how higher education checks that: 47 questions in 9 sections, each answered with evidence. Here is every platform on this page put through the same review on November 18, 2026, highest first. Pick a column heading to sort by it.
