How HAX Lab keeps HAX going

Class feeds clubs, clubs feed the lab, the lab feeds the product, and graduates come back as mentors.

IST 256 at Penn State teaches web development on real HAX problems. Class projects often become HAX blocks. One term, 36 students filed 24 UX findings and 18 were fixed before the term ended.

There are many more: every IST 256 story on hax.cloud

Student clubs bring their own passions. The cyber security club ran two long audits and helped fix 30 vulnerabilities. The UX club applied their training to the editor.

There are many more: every community story on hax.cloud

One-term internships in Arts and Architecture. Each student owns one real project: a theme, the CLI, the design system, the dashboard.

There are many more: every HAX Lab story on hax.cloud

Faculty fund student work that solves their own problems, and HAX gains the capability: portfolios students take with them, and Canvas course analysis.

There are many more: every Penn State story on hax.cloud

Students leave with shipped work, not just a grade. Some return as teaching assistants and mentors for the next group.

There are many more: every win on hax.cloud
