Motion on the icon's own hover or keyboard focus

Decorative only: plays once, and not at all when reduced motion is turned on. The last icon has no motion and never moves.

Played by a parent: ddd-motion-trigger

Hovering the button, or moving keyboard focus to it, plays the motion of the icon inside.

Played by a parent: motion-active

A parent that tracks its own state sets motion-active on the icon while it should play.
