Our voice stays the same everywhere. Tone shifts with the moment: encouraging in a lesson, calm in an error, brief in a button.

Guidelines

Use "you" and speak to a single person. Avoid "users" and "learners" in the text a learner reads.

Put the answer or the action first, then the detail. Most readers stop after the first sentence.

An error message names the problem and the next step. It never blames the reader.

 Idioms rarely translate. "Hit the ground running" becomes "start right away".
