This is a quiet theme for documentation, handbooks and course notes. The site menu runs down the left, the page sits in the middle, and the headings of the page you are reading run down the right.

What you get

  • A site menu that follows your outline, with the active page marked
  • An on this page list built from the headings in the page
  • Search, light and dark mode, previous and next links
  • Every HAX block, styled by DDD

Small screens

On a phone the menu tucks behind a button in a short header, and the on this page list folds into a button above the page title.

Log in and the HAX editor, the top bar and the outline designer all work the way they do in any other theme.
