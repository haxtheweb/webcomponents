In a classroom, contact happens without anyone planning it. Online, none of that happens on its own. If you do not build contact into the course structure, many students will finish your entire course without a single meaningful interaction with you.

Frequent student-faculty contact in and out of classes is the most important factor in student motivation and involvement.

What this principle is asking

Instructor presence is not a nice to have. It is a fundamental driver of motivation, persistence, and learning.

What this looks like in practice

Record a short video at the start of each module connecting the week content to something current. Post a weekly announcement that does more than list deadlines. Hold office hours at multiple times across time zones.
