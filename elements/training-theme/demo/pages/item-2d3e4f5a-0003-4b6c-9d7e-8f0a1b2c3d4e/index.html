Ask of every part of your course: what are students doing with this material? Not receiving it. Not storing it. Doing something with it.

Learning is not a spectator sport.

What this principle is asking

Active learning means asking students to analyze, apply, create, evaluate, experiment, and reflect. In science this is not optional: science is a process of inquiry.

What this looks like in practice

Swap some readings for guided inquiry where students explore a phenomenon and hypothesize before reading. Use simulations to explore before formalizing the mathematics. Assign case studies from real published research.
