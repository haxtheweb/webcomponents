Science does not happen in isolation. Research is conducted in teams, data is peer reviewed, and discoveries build on the work of others. Yet the default structure of many online courses looks nothing like this.

Learning is enhanced when it is more like a team effort than a solo race.

What this principle is asking

If you want students to learn from each other, you have to design for it. It will not happen on its own.

What this looks like in practice

Assign persistent small groups with rotating roles. Use structured peer review on major assignments with a rubric focused on reasoning. Run a first week icebreaker tied to your discipline.
