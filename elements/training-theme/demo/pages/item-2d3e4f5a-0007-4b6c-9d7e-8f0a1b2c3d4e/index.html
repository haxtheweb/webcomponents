Your online courses serve a genuinely diverse population: multilingual learners, students with disabilities, students still building their math backgrounds. Respecting diverse talents means designing so all students have a real opportunity to succeed.

There are many roads to learning.

What this principle is asking

If your course design systematically disadvantages some students, then your grades are measuring design barriers as much as student learning. That is worth examining honestly.

What this looks like in practice

Caption all videos and provide transcripts. Audit materials for accessibility before the course begins. Offer alternative formats for some major assignments. Include a proactive accommodations statement in your syllabus.
