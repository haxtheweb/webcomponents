A student opens the syllabus on day one. Does she know how many hours a week this requires? Does she have enough information to decide, honestly, whether she can manage this alongside her job and her kids?

Time plus energy equals learning. There is no substitute for time on task.

What this principle is asking

Designing with time on task in mind is a form of respect for the lives your students actually live.

What this looks like in practice

Include a weekly time commitment estimate in your syllabus. Add time to completion estimates to each module. Flag heavy weeks in advance and set deadlines for when working adults are actually free.
