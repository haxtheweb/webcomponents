A student submits a lab report. Three weeks later, a number appears. Two words in the comments: needs improvement. Compare that with a short audio note walking through exactly where the reasoning broke down and what to do differently.

Knowing what you know and do not know focuses learning.

What this principle is asking

The difference is the difference between feedback that teaches and feedback that scores. Online, every bit of it has to be deliberately designed.

What this looks like in practice

State a response time policy and honor it. Use rubrics so criteria are transparent before submission. Try short audio feedback for complex assignments. Build practice quizzes with detailed answer explanations.
