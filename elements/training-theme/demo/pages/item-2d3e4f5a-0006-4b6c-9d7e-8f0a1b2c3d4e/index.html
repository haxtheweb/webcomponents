You know what excellent scientific work looks like. Your students, especially early on, often do not. They are not being lazy when they aim too low. They are guessing.

Expect more and you will get more.

What this principle is asking

High expectations do not mean making things harder. They mean making the target explicit.

What this looks like in practice

Share an annotated example of excellent work with commentary on what makes it strong. Write objectives as what students will be able to do. Provide detailed rubrics before students begin working.
