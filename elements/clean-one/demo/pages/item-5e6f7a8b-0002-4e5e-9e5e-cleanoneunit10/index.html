Unit 1: Foundations

This unit introduces the fundamentals of open education and how open licensing changes the way courses are built and shared.

Learning Objectives

By the end of this unit, you will be able to:

  • Define open educational resources and open pedagogy.
  • Explain the five rights that make up the open licensing framework.
  • Identify how a course outline maps to navigation and page structure.
