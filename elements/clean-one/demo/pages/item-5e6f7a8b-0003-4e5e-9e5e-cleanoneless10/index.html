What is OER?

Open educational resources, or OER, are teaching and learning materials that are free to access and openly licensed so that anyone can adapt them for their own context.

The Five Rights

The classic framing of open licensing includes five rights, sometimes called the five Rs:

  • Retain the right to make, own, and control copies of the content.
  • Reuse the content in a wide range of ways.
  • Revise the content to adapt and modify it.
  • Remix the original or revised content with other material.
  • Redistribute copies and adaptations to others.
