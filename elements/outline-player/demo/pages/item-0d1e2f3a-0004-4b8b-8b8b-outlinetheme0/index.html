Working with Themes

The appearance of a site is controlled by its theme, and the content stays untouched when the theme changes. outline-player itself is one such theme, tuned for documentation and outlined content.

What a Theme Controls

  • The chrome around the content, such as the navigation drawer, header controls, and breadcrumbs.
  • The typography and spacing applied to page content.
  • Light and dark presentation, which follows the system preference.

Because themes are just web components, swapping one in the outline changes the whole presentation without rewriting a single page.
