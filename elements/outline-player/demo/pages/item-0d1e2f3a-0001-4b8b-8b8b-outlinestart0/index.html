Getting Started

This documentation site is rendered by outline-player, a HAXcms theme built for presenting outlined content. The navigation drawer on the left mirrors the site outline, the content you are reading lives in the center column, and controls for search, print, and previous and next pages sit above the article.

Getting Around

  • Use the navigation drawer to jump between any page in the outline.
  • Use the previous and next buttons to walk the outline in order.
  • Use the menu button to collapse or expand the drawer on small screens.

Continue to the User Guides section to see how nested pages appear in the drawer and in the breadcrumb trail.
