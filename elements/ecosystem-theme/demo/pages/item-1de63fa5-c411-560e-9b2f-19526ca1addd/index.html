HAX ecosystem

  1. Humans are at the center of HAX.
  2. People hold the seven pillars that make it powerful.
  3. The Future Traveler is your AI companion, working alongside you.
  4. It began at Penn State, with ELMS:LN and the people who built it.
  5. Penn State backs the work through its colleges and University Libraries.
  6. You build with HAXcms on PHP, Node.js, the CLI or HAX11ty.
  7. Authoring happens in the editor, Merlin, themes and the dashboard; open HAXcms for every admin piece.
  8. The building blocks rest on the web component standard.
  9. Schemas are the contracts that keep every part portable.
  10. Skeletons make courses, blogs, portfolios and department sites reusable.
  11. HAX Lab and student clubs bring design and security; HAX Camp and DrupalCon grow the community.
  12. Students and faculty built much of what you see.
  13. The traveler learns from PRAW, skills, the MCP and the issue queue, checked by automated tests.
  14. IST 256 turns students into contributors; grants fund the work.
  15. Apereo, GitHub and npm connect HAX to the open source world; Buttercups Training contributed from the UK.
  16. Ubiquity, Lit SSR and deploy targets ship it anywhere, fast.
  17. WINS keeps the proof: the people and groups it helped.
  18. One web of connections. Every part makes every other part stronger.
