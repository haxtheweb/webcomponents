# webcomponents Roadmap

Priorities are set in the unified issue queue (haxtheweb/issues); larger efforts get a plan posted as an issue comment and labeled `Plan Created`. The ecosystem-level roadmap lives at https://github.com/haxtheweb/issues/blob/master/ROADMAP.md

## How to influence this roadmap

Open an issue in the unified queue. Teaching and use-case issues get weighed directly against these lines.
