# Trademark and Brand Usage

This repository follows the HAX trademark and brand usage policy (source of truth for `haxtheweb/*`):
https://github.com/haxtheweb/issues/blob/master/TRADEMARK.md

The policy covers acceptable use of the HAX name and logo, what counts as an official release vs. a redistribution, and how vendors and hosting partners may describe their relationship to HAX.

Questions about brand use: open an issue in the unified queue https://github.com/haxtheweb/issues/issues
